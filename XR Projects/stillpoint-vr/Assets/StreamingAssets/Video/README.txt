Optional real 360° footage.

Drop an equirectangular (2:1) MP4 here named after a scene id to replace that
scene's procedural world:

  ocean.mp4   forest.mp4   rain.mp4   stars.mp4   waterfall.mp4

Tip for phones: H.264, 3840x1920 or 2880x1440, 30 fps, under ~150 MB.
If the image looks mirrored, set _FlipU = 1 on the Stillpoint/Video360 material in Environment.cs.
