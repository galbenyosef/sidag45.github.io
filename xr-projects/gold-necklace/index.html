IAlloy & Melt合金熔煉

Pure gold is too soft to wear every day.

24K gold is blended with copper and silver (palladium for white gold), then melted past 1,000 °C into 18K bars for daily wear.

IIRoll & Anneal軋延退火

Rolled into a rod, then softened.

The bar passes through a rolling mill until it is a long, even rod. Cold metal cracks, so it goes into the oven, cools slowly, and is ready to stretch.

IIIDraw the Wire拉絲

Drawn finer than silk.

The rod is pulled through diamond-lined draw plates, each hole smaller than the last, until it becomes one unbroken wire fine enough to link by hand.

IVHand-Link手工鏈環

Every ring cut and interlocked.

The wire is wrapped around a rod and cut into individual rings. Each one is woven into the next by hand, building the cable pattern link by link.

Each joint fused by flame.

Wax carved, gold poured, a moss agate set.
