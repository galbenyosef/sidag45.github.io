  1. Site review

    A free 30-minute call. We look at your site together and tell you where 3D or gesture control would help sales, and where it wouldn't.

  2. Prototype

    A working demo on a private test page of your own site, so you can try it on your phone before committing to the full build.

  3. Build and integrate

  4. Launch and measure

Questions owners ask us

Will 3D slow my website down?

Not if it's built properly. Models are compressed and load only when they scroll into view, after everything else on the page. Phones on slower connections get a lighter version.

Does it work with Shopify, Webflow or WordPress?

Yes. We deliver an embed that fits your platform and install it for you. Custom-built sites work too.

What happens to camera video from my customers?

Nothing leaves their device. Tracking runs inside the browser, and the page only sees the positions of points, never the picture. Visitors always choose whether to turn the camera on.

What if a visitor's device can't show 3D or use a camera?

They see a normal image gallery and normal buttons. Every interactive piece we build has a plain fallback, so no one is locked out.

I only have photos of my products. Is that enough?

Usually, yes. A set of photos from different angles is enough to build an accurate model of most products. We'll tell you on the first call if we need anything else.
