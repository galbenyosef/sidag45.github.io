The Lanterns燈苑

Twenty-four homes on the hillside above Pok Fu Lam, one to a floor, every one facing the East Lamma Channel.

Book a viewing Scroll to go up the building

One home on every floor

Each floor is a single flat with glass on all four sides. There are no shared corridors and no neighbours through the wall. The lift opens straight into your hall.

Terraces that take turns

The terrace swaps corners on every floor, south-east then south-west. Nobody looks down onto the terrace below, and every one of them is open to the sky.

At night the lit floors stack up like paper lanterns on the hill, which is where the building gets its name. Try the night view at the top of the page.
