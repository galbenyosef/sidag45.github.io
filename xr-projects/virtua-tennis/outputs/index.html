How to play
AUTO-RUN
Your player reads the incoming shot and moves into position automatically. Swing as the ball bounces toward you — the shot is held for the contact point if you're a touch early. Swipe left for topspin, right for slice, up for flat, or down for a drop shot. Turn auto-run off to steer with your hand position or A / D.
✦  Face the camera and keep your racket hand visible. A relaxed, natural swing works best.
