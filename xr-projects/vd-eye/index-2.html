Eye gestures

Look
The red pointer goes where you look and settles on the nearest button or file
Blink
One deliberate blink is a left click. Quick natural blinks are ignored
Blink twice
Double-click: opens a folder or file
Long blink
Keep your eyes closed until you hear the rising tone (about 1 s) to press and hold. Look where to go, then blink to drop
Wink
Close one eye for a moment to right-click
Window edge
Look at the top or bottom edge of a window to scroll it
Top-right corner
Look into the corner for a second for Mission Control
