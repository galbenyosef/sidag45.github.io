Lend the choir your voice

Sing or speak for a few seconds. A long open sound like “aaah” or “ooo” works best. You can add a word or two after it, like hello in your language.

In this prototype your recording never leaves this browser. It's checked and analysed on your device, sung while this page is open, and gone when you close it. You can withdraw it at any time.

Record a few seconds

Hold one open note for a couple of seconds, then say a word if you like. The choir goes quiet while you record.
