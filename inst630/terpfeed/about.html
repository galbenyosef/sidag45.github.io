About

Why TerpFeed exists

UMD students get updates from at least four places: Canvas for classes, TerpLink for clubs, GroupMe for teams and email for everything else. Each one has its own notifications and its own habits, so important updates get missed and students end up muting everything.

TerpFeed puts those updates in one feed. Students follow the groups they care about. Group admins post once, and followers see it in their feed, with important posts highlighted at the top.

What makes it low-noise

  • You choose what to follow. Nothing shows up in your feed unless you follow the group.
  • Important posts are limited. Admins can mark only two posts per week as Important, so the highlight keeps its meaning.
  • Per-group notification levels. Set each group to All, Important only or Mute.
  • Events go to your calendar. RSVP and add events to Google or Outlook Calendar in one click.

Who it's for

Students who want one place to see what matters. Group admins such as club officers, team captains, TAs and professors who need to reach members reliably. Moderators who verify class admins and review reports.

About this project

TerpFeed is a final project for INST630 at the University of Maryland. It is a front-end prototype: groups and posts come from sample JSON data, and your account, follows, saved posts and RSVPs are stored in your browser with localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server.
